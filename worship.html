Worship at Oxon Hill Church of Christ

At the Oxon Hill Church of Christ, we strive to worship God in spirit and in truth (John 4:24), following the teachings and examples found in the New Testament. We invite you to join us as we come together to praise God, study His Word, and encourage one another in the faith.

Our Worship

What to Expect During Worship

Select a card to read more and see the Scripture passages from the American Standard Version (ASV, 1901).

01 Singing A cappella singing, praising God and teaching one another through psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs.

We sing together as a congregation, offering praise to God and encouraging one another through the words we sing.

Ephesians 5:19 (ASV)

speaking one to another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody with your heart to the Lord;

Colossians 3:16 (ASV)

Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly; in all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another with psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts unto God.

02 Prayer Offering prayers of thanksgiving, supplication, and intercession.

We approach God in prayer to give thanks, make requests, and pray for others.

Acts 2:42 (ASV)

And they continued stedfastly in the apostles' teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread and the prayers.

1 Timothy 2:1–2 (ASV)

I exhort therefore, first of all, that supplications, prayers, intercessions, thanksgivings, be made for all men; for kings and all that are in high place; that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and gravity.

03 Preaching and Teaching Studying and proclaiming God's Word, stirring one another up to love and good works, and growing in understanding and faith.

We seek to teach the Scriptures faithfully, helping one another understand God's Word and put it into practice.

2 Timothy 4:2 (ASV)

preach the word; be urgent in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all longsuffering and teaching.

Hebrews 10:24 (ASV)

and let us consider one another to provoke unto love and good works;

04 The Lord's Supper Remembering the sacrifice of Jesus Christ through the breaking of bread and partaking of the cup.

We remember the Lord's death through the bread and the cup, following the New Testament example of the disciples gathering to break bread.

Acts 20:7 (ASV)

And upon the first day of the week, when we were gathered together to break bread, Paul discoursed with them, intending to depart on the morrow; and prolonged his speech until midnight.

1 Corinthians 11:26 (ASV)

For as often as ye eat this bread, and drink the cup, ye proclaim the Lord's death till he come.

05 Giving Giving willingly and purposefully to support the work of the church.

We give as we have purposed in our hearts, recognizing the importance of cheerful and willing giving.

1 Corinthians 16:2 (ASV)

Upon the first day of the week let each one of you lay by him in store, as he may prosper, that no collections be made when I come.

2 Corinthians 9:7 (ASV)

Let each man do according as he hath purposed in his heart: not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

Scripture quotations: American Standard Version (1901), public domain in the United States.

Our Faith and Practice

What We Believe

Seeking to teach God's Word faithfully and live according to His truth.

Our Foundation Is God's Word

We believe that all Scripture is inspired by God and that we are called not only to teach His Word, but also to live according to it, continually growing in faith and in the knowledge of His truth (2 Timothy 3:16–17; James 1:22; 2 Peter 3:18).

While the Old Testament was written for our learning (Romans 15:4), we recognize that we are now under the New Covenant established through Jesus Christ (Hebrews 9:15–17). The teachings of Jesus Christ and His inspired apostles serve as our authority in matters of faith and practice, guiding us in understanding and obeying the truth God has revealed through His Word.

01 Hear the Word of God

Faith comes through hearing the Word of God. We must first hear the message of Christ to understand what God calls us to believe and obey.

Romans 10:17 (ASV)

So belief cometh of hearing, and hearing by the word of Christ.

02 Believe That Jesus Is the Christ

We must believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and trust in Him as the One through whom salvation is offered.

John 3:16 (ASV)

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth on him should not perish, but have eternal life.

John 8:24 (ASV)

I said therefore unto you, that ye shall die in your sins: for except ye believe that I am he, ye shall die in your sins.

03 Repent of Our Sins

Repentance is a change of heart and direction: turning away from sin and turning toward God in obedience to His will.

Luke 13:3 (ASV)

I tell you, Nay: but, except ye repent, ye shall all in like manner perish.

Acts 17:30 (ASV)

The times of ignorance therefore God overlooked; but now he commandeth men that they should all everywhere repent:

04 Confess That Jesus Christ Is the Son of God

Our faith in Christ is not something to conceal. We openly confess our belief in Jesus Christ and acknowledge Him as Lord.

Romans 10:9 (ASV)

because if thou shalt confess with thy mouth Jesus as Lord, and shalt believe in thy heart that God raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved:

Romans 10:10 (ASV)

for with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.

05 Be Baptized for the Remission of Sins

The Scriptures connect baptism with calling on the Lord, the washing away of sins, and salvation. We teach baptism as part of the response of faith and obedience to the gospel.

Acts 22:16 (ASV)

And now why tarriest thou? arise, and be baptized, and wash away thy sins, calling on his name.

1 Peter 3:21 (ASV)

which also after a true likeness doth now save you, even baptism, not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the interrogation of a good conscience toward God, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ;

Mark 16:16 (ASV)

He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that disbelieveth shall be condemned.

06 Remain Faithful Unto Death

Following Christ is a lifelong commitment. We strive to remain faithful to Him, even in the face of hardship, with the hope of the crown of life.

Revelation 2:10 (ASV)

Fear not the things which thou art about to suffer: behold, the devil is about to cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days. Be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee the crown of life.

Scripture quotations are from the American Standard Version (ASV), 1901, public domain in the United States.

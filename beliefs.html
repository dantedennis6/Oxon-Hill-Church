Our Faith and Practice

What We Believe

Seeking to teach God's Word faithfully and live according to His truth.

Our Foundation Is God's Word

We believe that all Scripture is inspired by God and that we are called not only to teach His Word, but also to live according to it, continually growing in faith and in the knowledge of His truth (2 Timothy 3:16–17; James 1:22; 2 Peter 3:18).

While the Old Testament was written for our learning (Romans 15:4), we recognize that we are now under the New Covenant established through Jesus Christ (Hebrews 9:15–17). The teachings of Jesus Christ and His inspired apostles serve as our authority in matters of faith and practice, guiding us in understanding and obeying the truth God has revealed through His Word.

01 Hear the Word of God Unread

Faith comes through hearing the Word of God. We must first hear the message of Christ to understand what God calls us to believe and obey.

Romans 10:17 (KJV)

So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

02 Believe That Jesus Is the Christ Unread

We must believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and trust in Him as the One through whom salvation is offered.

John 3:16 (KJV)

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

John 8:24 (KJV)

I said therefore unto you, that ye shall die in your sins: for if ye believe not that I am he, ye shall die in your sins.

03 Repent of Our Sins Unread

Repentance is a change of heart and direction: turning away from sin and turning toward God in obedience to His will.

Luke 13:3 (KJV)

I tell you, Nay: but, except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish.

Acts 17:30 (KJV)

And the times of this ignorance God winked at; but now commandeth all men every where to repent:

04 Confess That Jesus Christ Is the Son of God Unread

Our faith in Christ is not something to conceal. We openly confess our belief in Jesus Christ and acknowledge Him as Lord.

Romans 10:9 (KJV)

That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.

Romans 10:10 (KJV)

For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.

05 Be Baptized for the Remission of Sins Unread

The Scriptures teach that baptism is full immersion in water, representing our burial and resurrection with Jesus Christ (Romans 6:3–4). The Scriptures connect baptism with calling on the Lord, the washing away of sins, and salvation. We teach baptism as part of the response of faith and obedience to the gospel.

Acts 22:16 (KJV)

And now why tarriest thou? arise, and be baptized, and wash away thy sins, calling on the name of the Lord.

1 Peter 3:21 (KJV)

The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ:

Mark 16:16 (KJV)

He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned.

06 Remain Faithful Unto Death Unread

Following Christ is a lifelong commitment. We strive to remain faithful to Him, even in the face of hardship, with the hope of the crown of life.

Revelation 2:10 (KJV)

Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.

Scripture quotations are from the King James Version (KJV), public domain in the United States.

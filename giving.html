GIVING WITH A CHEERFUL HEART

Giving

An opportunity to give willingly, cheerfully, and according to the purpose of our hearts.

WHAT THE SCRIPTURES TEACH

“Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.”

2 Corinthians 9:7 (KJV)

A BIBLICAL PRINCIPLE

The Purpose of Giving

Giving is an important part of our worship and service to God.

At Oxon Hill Church of Christ, we believe giving should be done willingly and cheerfully, as taught in the Scriptures.

Each person is encouraged to give according to what they have purposed in their heart, rather than out of reluctance or compulsion.

The contributions of the congregation help support the work of the church, including the teaching of God's Word, evangelism, and other congregational needs.

01

Willingly

Giving should come from a willing heart, not from pressure or compulsion.

02

Cheerfully

God loves a cheerful giver. Our giving should reflect gratitude and sincerity.

03

Purposefully

Each person should thoughtfully determine what they intend to give.
